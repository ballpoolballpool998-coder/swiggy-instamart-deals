Frequently Asked Questions & Tips

How do I choose specific subcategories?

Click on any category card to view its subcategories. Click individual subcategory pills to toggle them on or off, or use the "Select All" / "Clear" buttons.

Why choose fewer subcategories?

Scanning 2 to 4 aisles completes in only 1–2 seconds and completely eliminates 429 WAF rate limit challenges from Swiggy.

Can I still fetch the entire category?

Yes! Clicking the category header automatically selects all subcategories in that category so you can scan the whole department in one click.

How does the single-category rule work?

Selecting subcategories in a new category automatically switches your focus to that category, ensuring clean and accurate local dark store checks.
