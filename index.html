3

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this work for my area?

Yes. The bookmarklet detects your active delivery address and dark store ID from your browser session on swiggy.com automatically.

Why allow pop-ups?

Results open in a clean, standalone dashboard tab so you can search, filter, and add deals to your cart without losing your place.

Are personal cookies needed?

No. Both bookmarklets operate on public catalog data as anonymous guest queries. Your personal Swiggy account is never touched.
