Frequently Asked Questions & Tips

How do I choose specific subcategories?

Click on any category card to expand its subcategories. Click individual subcategory pills to toggle them on or off, or use the "Select All" / "Clear" buttons.

Why choose fewer subcategories?

Scanning 2 to 4 aisles completes in only 1–2 seconds and completely eliminates 429 WAF rate limit challenges from Swiggy.

Can I still fetch the entire category?

Yes! Clicking the radio button next to the category name automatically selects all subcategories in that department.

How does the brand filter work?

In the results tab, use the "All Brands" dropdown to instantly narrow down items to your favorite brands (e.g. Aashirvaad, Fortune, Amul).
